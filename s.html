Share channels

Channels for Ferrite radios as a QR code or a link. Scan it with Ferrite Pocket on a phone and the channels are added to your radio, at free channel numbers, so nothing you have changes.

Open in Ferrite Pocket

01Make a code

For a club net, a hamfest or a conference: type the frequencies, then put the QR code on your website, a slide or a badge. Two or more channels go as a zone, named below.

02Use it

What's in a code

Only the channels: names, frequencies, mode, tones, shift and power, any site positions, and the zone's name. No callsign and nothing else from a radio. The code is the part of the link after the #, which a browser never sends to a website: this page reads and makes codes in your browser, and nothing is stored.

Each channel goes at the first free number on the radio that adds it; tones and power can be changed there afterwards. The radio refuses anything outside its bands or its region's transmit rules, as it does any channel.
