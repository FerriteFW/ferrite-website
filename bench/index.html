04Radio manufacturers

We would be glad to work with radio manufacturers. Hardware documentation, schematics or sample units would let Ferrite support more radios and do it properly, and a manufacturer who wants Ferrite on its radios can license it from us. Either way, Ferrite stays free for the people who own the radios.

And we would love to help spec the next generation of hacker-friendly, open radios: the parts, the pins and the documentation that make a radio easy to build on, from what we've learned on this one.
