This is the Ferrite blog. Ferrite is free firmware for the Radtel RT-950 Pro, with its source public, and this is
where we'll write about it.

What goes here:

- Releases: what's new in each one, and anything to know before you update.
- Notes from the bench: what we measured, and how.
- How a feature came to work the way it does.

To follow along, add the [feed]({{ site.urls.blog }}/feed.xml) to your reader. For questions, the
[Ferrite Discord]({{ site.discord }}) is the place.
