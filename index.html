Ferrite

Free firmware for the Radtel RT-950 Pro, and radios built on the same hardware. Written from scratch, with its source public.

See what it does Download

Three slots, one radio

Talk on the slot in front while the other two keep listening. SLOT brings the next one forward.

The band scope

Hold 1 to see what's on the air around you. The knob moves the marker, and the strongest signal is named below.

APRS on slot C

Slot C decodes APRS in the background while A and B stay yours for voice. Its card shows who was heard, how far away, and new messages.

Crossband repeater

What's heard on one band goes out on the other, and back. Who's passing, the timeout and the last 30 minutes, live.

Shortwave

The broadcast receiver also tunes shortwave and the HF bands, in AM, on its own screen.

Low Power Mode

One switch keeps only what you need to talk on one frequency. Hold SIDE1 and SIDE2 to get everything back.
